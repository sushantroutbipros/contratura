Sub-contractors

For IOCL, a sub-contractor is part of you. If the EIC approves it, it sits inside the contract file. In most real cases it is unofficial. Either way, you answer for its work and its delay, and any dispute with it is settled between you and the sub-contractor. GCC 4.8 M-12 proposed

How IOCL sees a sub-contractor. It is your servant or agent. You are exclusively responsible for its acts, omissions and defaults (GCC 4.8.2.0). Its breakdowns are your delay, and no extension of time is open for them.
How disputes run. The IOCL route on the Disputes tab covers you and IOCL only. A dispute with your sub-contractor follows its own order: its terms, a settlement, then the forum named there. Money you owe it, and money it owes you, never reach IOCL.
